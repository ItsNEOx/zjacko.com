Connect

Link this site to your LinkedIn account — owner only.

Checking the connection…

The LINKEDIN_ADMIN_KEY password from .env. It keeps anyone else from pointing your feed at their own LinkedIn, and is kept in this tab only (sessionStorage) — it is never written anywhere else.

Connect runs LinkedIn's official OAuth consent screen: you log in as yourself, click Allow, and this server stores the token it receives in private/ — the browser never sees it. Tokens last 60 days; when one expires, come back and click Connect once more (while you are still logged in to LinkedIn, it's a single click with no consent screen). The author URN is discovered automatically.

Reading your posts needs LinkedIn's approval. Connect requests the scopes r_member_social openid profile. openid + profile (your live name and photo) work right away. r_member_social — the posts themselves — is a restricted scope: request the Community Management API product on your LinkedIn app and LinkedIn must approve it. Until then the feed stays in its fallback state. Full steps are in README.md → “Connecting your LinkedIn”.
