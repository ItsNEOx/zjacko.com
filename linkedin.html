LinkedIn

Profile

The professional side of things.

The profile card is rendered by <linkedin-card> from data/linkedin.json — edit that file to update the summary, skills, and experience shown here. When the companion Node server (server.js) is running, it also swaps in your live LinkedIn profile picture and name, and the feed below pulls your real posts (text, photos, and permalinks) read-only from LinkedIn. To link your account once, open connect.html and click Connect (see README.md for the one-time LinkedIn app setup). Run npm start, then open this page via http://localhost:3000 so the site and API share one origin. Nothing on this page can publish to LinkedIn.
