Profile

The professional side of things.

The profile card is rendered by <linkedin-card> from data/linkedin.json — edit that file to update the summary, skills, and experience shown here. The feed below is a local, read-only clone of your LinkedIn posts: data/posts.json, synced from LinkedIn's official "Download your data" export with node tools/import-shares.js, or one post at a time with node tools/add-post.js (see README.md). It renders on any host with no server, and nothing on this page can publish to LinkedIn.
