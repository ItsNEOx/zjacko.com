LinkedIn

Profile

The professional side of things.

The profile card is rendered by <linkedin-card> from data/linkedin.json, the certifications tab by <linkedin-certifications>, and the feed by <linkedin-posts> from data/posts.json. Both files are synced word-for-word from Zach's own LinkedIn profile every 4 hours (see README.md). The feed shows the 5 newest posts; the rest are on LinkedIn. Everything is static and read-only — nothing on this page can publish to LinkedIn.
